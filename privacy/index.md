# Privacy

Updated on November 13, 2026. This is a translation: the [Portuguese version](https://genesiskore.com/privacidade/) is the official one.

This page explains what GenesisKore does with the data you enter in the diagnosis form on this site, and what your rights are under Brazil's General Data Protection Law ([LGPD, Law 13,709/2018](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)).

## Who looks after the data

GenesisKore, based in São Paulo, Brazil, is in the process of incorporation. For anything about your data, write to [contato@genesiskore.com](mailto:contato@genesiskore.com).

## What we collect

What you type in the form: name, company, WhatsApp or email and, if you want, the company's revenue range and the task that takes most of your team's time. It also records where you came to the site from (for example, Instagram, LinkedIn or a referral).

If you take the quick diagnosis on the home page, the answers stay in your browser. They only reach us in two ways: if you send the form, in which case they go with your message and are shown before sending so you can check or remove them, or if you ask for the X-ray report in PDF.

To build the X-ray report, we receive the six answers, your name, company, email and, if you want, your WhatsApp. The report is built by fixed rules, with no artificial intelligence, and sits at a link that only someone with the address can open, for 90 days. The revenue range goes into the calculation but does not appear in the report. To block automated submissions, we keep a scrambled mark of your internet address, which cannot be turned back into the address.

The site also counts visits, with no cookies and without storing your IP. Each page view sends the page visited, the site you came from (only the site name), the campaign tags in the link, whether it is a phone or a computer, and a random code that lasts only while the tab is open. None of this identifies you. The site uses no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking tools.

## What we use it for

- To answer your request and book the diagnosis conversation, by WhatsApp or email.
- To build your X-ray report, when you ask for it.
- To send the email confirming that your request arrived.
- To prepare the conversation: an artificial intelligence tool summarizes what you wrote, so our team arrives knowing what it is about.
- To know how many people open each page and where they come from, without knowing who they are.
The legal basis is your consent, given in the form's checkbox, and the steps that come before a possible contract.

## Who the data stays with

The data goes through the services we use to operate: the automation server (hosted at Hostinger), the database of our internal platform (Supabase), email (Google) and the artificial intelligence service that writes the summary. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

## For how long

For as long as we are in conversation with you. If the conversation does not become a project, the data is deleted within 12 months of the last contact, or sooner if you ask.

## Your rights

At any time you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, know who it was shared with, or withdraw your consent. Just write to [contato@genesiskore.com](mailto:contato@genesiskore.com). We answer within 15 days.
