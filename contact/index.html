São Paulo, SP, Brazil. The work is done remotely or on site when needed, for companies anywhere in Brazil, in Portuguese, English or Spanish.

What happens next

In the diagnosis conversation you show how the work happens today, and you leave knowing what is worth automating and what isn't, even if you don't hire us. If it makes sense, a fixed-price proposal follows, in three options, with a written list of what the system will do. If the question is about margin, cash or processes, the conversation is about the Management Consulting, and the proposal comes with scope and deadline in writing.

Before you write

It helps a lot to know two things: the company's approximate monthly revenue and the task that takes up most of the team's time today. With that we already come to the conversation knowing whether it makes sense, and we say so right away if it doesn't.
