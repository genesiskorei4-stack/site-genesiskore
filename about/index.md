# About GenesisKore

Updated on November 13, 2026.

The company grew and still depends on you. GenesisKore builds custom systems and automations, and organizes the management behind them, for small and mid-sized Brazilian companies that have already made it and still depend on the owner, or on a single person, for everything to work.

The company was founded in August 2025 in São Paulo and serves all of Brazil, remotely or on site when needed. The team is small and, on larger projects, works with partner developers. Conversations and proposals can be in Portuguese, English or Spanish.

## What does GenesisKore do?

We take repetitive work off the team's hands. Orders checked, documents issued, invoices sent, customers told about their delivery, the monthly report ready in the inbox. Often this runs behind the WhatsApp, e-mail and spreadsheets the company already uses. When a new screen is needed, it is made for that person's job and opens on the phone.

The other half of the work is showing the money: how much is left over per product, per customer and per channel, and whether the month closes.

## Who is it for?

For the owner whose company has revenue from about R$ 150 thousand per month and who wants to step out of the day-to-day operation. The sectors where we work most are distribution and wholesale, high-value retail and service firms such as law, accounting and engineering offices.

What we don't do: chatbots that talk to customers on WhatsApp, software for clinics and healthcare, and institutional websites. And if the system you already pay for solves your problem, we tell you that in the first conversation, with no proposal at all.

## Management consulting

Since October 2026 GenesisKore has a second product, GenesisKore Management Consulting: managerial accounting, financial analysis, processes, market research, economic scenarios and customer experience, so the owner decides with numbers. The people who diagnose sit at the same table as the people who build the system. [See the Management Consulting](https://genesiskore.com/management-consulting/).

## Who are the partners?

GenesisKore was founded in August 2025 by Felipe Concilio and Gustavo Cossignani. In October 2026, with the Management Consulting product, Vitor Costa and João Cavalcanti joined as partners. In Consulting, the four of them work together on every project.

### Felipe Concilio

Co-founder · systems and marketing

Builds the systems and automations GenesisKore delivers, from the screen the team uses to what runs behind it. He also runs the company's marketing and shares the finances with João.

### Gustavo Cossignani

Co-founder · sales

He is the first person the owner talks to. He leads the diagnosis conversation, writes the proposal and follows the sale until it is signed. With Vitor, he decides what goes into each product.

### Vitor Costa

Partner · projects and processes

He is responsible for projects: scope, schedule and on-time delivery. He comes from FIA Júnior, where he is a consulting project specialist, and heads processes at FIA Startups.

### João Cavalcanti

Partner · finance and numbers

He looks after the numbers: margin, cost and cash for Consulting clients, and the company's own finances together with Felipe. He studies Economics at FIA, presides over FIA Startups and works on sales alongside Gustavo.

## How does GenesisKore work?

First a diagnosis conversation, which costs nothing. Then a fixed-price proposal, in three options, with a written list of what the system will do. The first part is in use within 2 weeks, and the rest comes in phases. After go-live we follow up, adjust and fix, and the system itself alerts our team when something fails.
