The main product: AI Consulting for your business

AI Consulting for your business

“Everybody talks about AI. I have no idea what it would do in here.”

What you get A map of which tasks AI can take over in your company, and which ones call for automation or a system. The AI set up the way your company works, a live class with your team and a follow-up 30 days later. With Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot. Usually three weeks.

What is usually missing is not a tool. The AI does not know the company, and the team does not know which task to use it for.

The four steps

  1. Understand the company. A ninety-minute conversation with the owner and a shorter one with up to two people from the team. Each area's week becomes a list of tasks: who does it, how long it takes and in which tool.
  2. Where AI comes in. Each task gets a destination: off-the-shelf AI solves it, a simple automation solves it, or it calls for a custom system. You get the map, ranked by hours saved for the effort, and the list of what not to buy.
  3. The AI ready to use. Our team builds the AI kit in your company's account and tests each assistant on real tasks.
  4. Class and follow-up. A two-hour live class with you and the team, recorded, using the kit on that week's work. Thirty days later, one hour to measure what was used and adjust what did not stick.

What comes in the AI kit

  • The company file. A document the AI reads before any conversation: what the company sells, to whom, the tone of voice, the terms of the trade and the rules on what never to promise.
  • Ready assistants, one per task in the map. Answering a quote request, writing a polite payment reminder, turning a meeting into a task list, reading the sales spreadsheet and pointing out what changed.
  • Skills. For Claude, the company's own skills (instruction files the AI picks up by itself when the task appears). In ChatGPT, the same thing goes in as custom GPTs.
  • Connectors. The AI linked to what the company already uses, such as Google Drive, Gmail and the calendar, when it makes sense. Read-only at first.
  • The prompt library. The prompts that worked on the real tasks, tested with the company's data.
  • Safe-use rules. What not to paste into the AI (customers' personal data, passwords, confidential contracts), the company account instead of personal accounts, and how to check an answer before it goes out. In line with LGPD, the Brazilian data protection law.

Not included: customer-service chatbots on WhatsApp, which GenesisKore does not build, and the AI tool subscription, which stays in the company's name. If all you want is to write better prompts, a good online course does that.

Questions

What is the difference between the Consulting and the Custom Systems?

The Custom Systems take the work that repeats every day off the team's hands. The Management Consulting gives the owner the number to decide: margin, cash, process, market. If what holds the company back is a task that repeats, it is a system. If it is a decision made without a number, it is consulting. In the first conversation we say which of the two makes sense, or neither.

Does the Consulting replace my accountant?

No. The accountant takes care of tax: bookkeeping, taxes and filings. The Consulting turns those numbers into decisions and works alongside the accountant.

Do I need to hire both?

No. Each one is hired on its own. When the analysis shows that a system solves part of the problem, we say so, and the decision is yours.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Whichever fits the company: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot or the AI already built into the software you use. If the tool you already pay for does the job, we set that one up and do not recommend another.

Do I need a custom system after the AI Consulting?

No. Many tasks are solved by off-the-shelf AI that is set up well, and the kit is built for that. When a task does call for a system, it shows up in the map with the reason, and becomes a separate proposal only if you want one.

How much does it cost?

There is no price list. The price comes in the proposal, together with the scope and the deadline in writing, after the first conversation, which costs nothing.
